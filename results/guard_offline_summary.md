## Findings

All numbers are decision level (the defense's own answer, warnings and hedge floor, no agent); verdicts use the
held-out bank. **Copy-paste (T0):** Guard removes most planted wins against page vote (k=5: 7% vs 62%; k=8: 17% vs
75%), and every remaining T0 planted win carries a DATA_VOID / CONTESTED warning (silent 0%). **Paraphrase (T1)
and cross-lingual (T3):** Guard helps little: T1 k=5 69% vs 76% (−7 pts, CI −14 to −1), k=8 97% vs 100%; T3 −6
and −4 pts. Guard counts a T1 campaign as about 5.4 independent origins out of 5.5 planted pages (T0: 1.0). This
is the outcome PREREG predicted before the test: the G2 page-vote conjunct passes on T0 only and fails on T1, so
G2 fails at decision level, and G1 is expected to fail on T1 (it needs no-defence agent runs to decide).
**Adaptive (T4):** the held-out bank has T4 for only 20 questions, all in the `known` regime, where every defense
makes the same decision (0% planted wins at k=3, 100% at k=8). Held-out T4 therefore cannot separate the
defenses, and the G6 "order holds" result is driven by T0 ≫ T1 ≈ T3 ≈ T4 (on the main bank T1 3.9% ≈ T3 4.0% ≈
T4 2.3% relative reduction). **Costs:** clean k=0 decision accuracy is unchanged against page vote (97.0%
held-out, 100% main). Honest false merges are 1.8% (held-out) and 0.5% (main), under G3's 5% bound. The gate
hedges 100% of honest single-origin and support pools, so G4 fails by construction (bound 40%). **Provenance
ablation:** guard and guard_noprov decisions differ by at most 1 pt on held-out (main T1 k=5: −4.2 pts), while
provenance edges raise honest false merges from 0.0% to 1.8%. **Urdu (exploratory):** no Guard effect on
held-out T1 (identical to page vote); −3.3 pts at k=3 on main. **Extraction does not generalise as it did on
dev (100% in-sample):** held-out English page slots 82.1% (unique pages 79.6%), Urdu 73.8%, main English 94.9%.
The weakest cells are held-out `place` answers (56%), cross-lingual T3 planted pages (43% held-out, 52% main)
and Urdu `name` answers (0–4%). Extraction errors feed every defense, so part of the held-out results (e.g.
single-origin accuracy 81%) reflects extraction rather than origin counting.
